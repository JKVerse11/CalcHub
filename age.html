How to use the Age Calculator

Select your date of birth. Your age is calculated relative to today using your device's local calendar date. Clear the date or use Reset values to start again.

Age calculation method

The calculator subtracts the birth date from today's date, then adjusts for whether the current month or day has passed the birthday. It accounts for differing month lengths and leap years. Total days is the elapsed number of calendar days between the two dates.

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator use today's date?

Yes. It uses the current date on your device, in its local time zone, whenever the page loads.

How are leap-year birthdays handled?

The age is calculated by comparing calendar years, months, and days. The result follows ordinary calendar date arithmetic; conventions for a February 29 birthday can vary by jurisdiction or purpose.

Is my date of birth stored?

The calculation runs in your browser and this page does not send the date to a server. See our Privacy Policy for general information about site services and hosting.
