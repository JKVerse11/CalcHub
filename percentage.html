How to use the Percentage Calculator

Enter the percentage and the number. For example, to find a 15% tip on 200, enter 15 and 200. The answer updates as you type, with the original inputs shown for reference.

Percentage formula

Percentage result = percentage × number ÷ 100. For instance, 15% of 200 = 15 × 200 ÷ 100 = 30.

Frequently asked questions

How do I find X% of a number?

Multiply the number by X, then divide by 100. This calculator performs that operation as soon as you enter both values.

Can I calculate a percentage greater than 100%?

Yes. Enter any positive or negative percentage and number; the calculator applies the same formula.

How can I work out a discount amount?

Enter the discount percentage and original price. The result is the amount saved; subtract it from the original price to get the discounted price.
