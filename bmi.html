How to use the BMI Calculator

Enter your weight in kilograms and height in centimetres. The body mass index and a general adult screening range update as you type. This tool is informational and does not assess health on its own.

BMI formula

BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ (height in metres × height in metres). For adults, commonly used screening categories are below 18.5, 18.5–24.9, 25.0–29.9, and 30 or more. These ranges are not a diagnosis and are not intended for children.

Frequently asked questions

What does BMI tell me?

BMI is a screening measure that compares weight with height. It does not directly measure body fat or account for all factors such as age, muscle mass, or individual health.

Are these BMI categories right for everyone?

No. Standard adult ranges may not apply to children, pregnant people, athletes, or every population. A healthcare professional can interpret results in context.

Can BMI diagnose a health condition?

No. BMI alone cannot diagnose a condition or determine an individual's health. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for advice, assessment, or treatment.
