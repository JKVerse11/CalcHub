How to use the GST Calculator

Enter an amount and the applicable GST rate. Choose “Add GST” if your amount is before tax, or “Remove GST” if the amount already includes GST. The tool shows the tax portion, base amount, and total.

GST formula

To add tax: GST = base amount × rate ÷ 100; total = base amount + GST. To extract tax from an inclusive amount: base = total ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100); GST = total − base. Tax rates and rules depend on the goods or services and applicable law.

Frequently asked questions

What GST rate should I use?

Use the rate that applies to the specific supply under current local tax rules. Rates can vary by product, service, and circumstances; verify with an official source or tax professional.

How do I remove GST from a price?

Select “Remove GST from total,” enter the tax-inclusive price, and supply its GST rate. The calculator backs out the tax using the inclusive-price formula.

Is this calculator an official tax tool?

No. It is a general arithmetic aid, not tax advice or an official filing tool. Confirm calculations and obligations with a qualified tax professional.
