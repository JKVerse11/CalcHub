How to use the SIP Calculator

Enter the monthly amount you plan to invest, an assumed annual rate of return, and how many years you expect to invest. The estimate updates as you type and separates your contributions from the projected growth.

SIP formula

For deposits at the beginning of each month, estimated future value = M × (((1 + r)n − 1) ÷ r) × (1 + r), where M is the monthly contribution, r is the assumed monthly return (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and n is the number of months. At zero return, future value equals monthly contribution × months.

Frequently asked questions

Are SIP returns guaranteed?

No. This is a mathematical illustration based on a constant assumed rate. Mutual fund and other investment returns vary and can be negative; the estimate is not a promise or recommendation.

What return rate should I enter?

Use an assumption appropriate for your own scenario and compare multiple rates. Past performance does not guarantee future results.

Does this calculator include taxes or fees?

No. Charges, taxes, inflation, missed contributions, and fluctuations in returns are not included. Your actual investment value may differ substantially.
