Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy explains how JKCalcHub handles information when you visit jkversetech.com. Review it before using the website. The operator should update this page whenever the site's services, advertising, or data practices change.

Information entered into calculators

Calculator inputs and calculations are processed in your browser by the website's JavaScript. JKCalcHub does not intentionally transmit calculator values to its own server. Avoid entering sensitive personal, health, or financial information. Your browser, hosting provider, network provider, or security tools may process technical request data as part of delivering the site.

Browser storage

The site stores your selected currency in your browser's local storage so the preference can be remembered. This is a local preference; it does not represent an exchange rate or convert the entered amount. You can clear it using your browser's site-data settings.

Hosting, fonts, and external services

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages and may load web fonts from Google Fonts. Those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when your browser requests their services. Consult their respective privacy documentation for details.

Advertising and cookies

At the time this policy was prepared, JKCalcHub has not added an advertising network or analytics script to the site. If advertising services such as Google AdSense are added, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, and personalize ads as described in their policies. This policy and any required consent mechanism must be updated before advertising is enabled. Users can review Google's information about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices and contact

You can disable cookies or clear local storage through your browser controls. Some site features may not retain preferences afterward. For privacy questions, contact jkversetech@gmail.com.
