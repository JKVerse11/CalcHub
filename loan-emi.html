How to use the Loan EMI Calculator

Enter the amount you plan to borrow, the annual interest rate quoted by your lender, and the loan tenure in years. Your estimated monthly EMI, total interest, and total payment update as you type. Actual lender schedules may differ because of fees, rate changes, and payment dates.

EMI formula

For a fixed-rate loan, EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1), where P is the principal, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100), and n is the total number of monthly instalments. At a zero interest rate, EMI is principal ÷ number of months.

Frequently asked questions

What does EMI mean?

EMI means Equated Monthly Instalment: a regular monthly payment that includes principal repayment and interest for a fixed-rate loan.

Does this estimate include fees or insurance?

No. It estimates principal and interest only. Processing fees, insurance, taxes, and other lender charges may increase your borrowing cost.

Will my EMI change during the loan?

This estimate assumes a constant rate and regular monthly payments. A floating-rate loan, prepayment, or lender-specific terms can change the actual schedule.
